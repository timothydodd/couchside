Couchside

A fast, lightweight media server for your movies, shows and live TV. It stays on your network, records like a real DVR, and looks good doing it.

Download Source on GitHub

Fast and light

One small binary with its database built in: a 7 MB download that idles in tens of megabytes. Happy on a NAS, a Raspberry Pi or a corner of a k3s cluster, and every page opens instantly.

Local only

Runs on your network with no Couchside account, no telemetry and nothing phoning home. It only reaches TMDB and the HDHomeRun guide, and caches what it fetches, so your devices talk to nobody but your server.

Live TV and a real DVR

Guide, one-off and series recordings, padding, watch while recording, and automatic commercial skipping. Recordings survive restarts, and broadcasts your TV can decode play without transcoding.

A UI worth using

A clean dark web app that works as well on a phone as on a desktop, a Plex-style player with subtitles, audio tracks and quality presets, and a Roku app for the couch.

Your library, matched

Point it at your folders: posters, backdrops, plots, cast and episode names, kept up to date as files come and go. A Manage view finds duplicates and low-quality copies.

Plays anything

Direct play when the device can, otherwise on-the-fly HLS on an Intel GPU, with HDR tone mapping.

For the whole household

Profiles keep their own progress, favourites and settings. Tap your profile to sign in at home, or set passwords. Admins decide who can record.

Run it

docker run -d --name couchside -p 8080:8080 \
  -v couchside-data:/data -v couchside-cache:/cache \
  -v /path/to/media:/media \
  ghcr.io/timothydodd/couchside:latest

Then open http://localhost:8080 and add your libraries. Zips for Linux, macOS and Windows, a Helm chart for Kubernetes and the full setup guide are in the install guide.
