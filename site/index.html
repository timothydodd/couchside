Couchside

Your movies, shows and live TV, served from your own server, to your browser and your Roku.

Download Source on GitHub

Your library, organised

Point it at your movie and TV folders. Couchside finds the titles, fetches posters, plots and episode names, and keeps up as files come and go.

Plays everything

Direct play when the device can, otherwise on-the-fly HLS with hardware transcoding, HDR tone mapping, subtitles and audio tracks.

Live TV and DVR

Watch and record from an HDHomeRun tuner, with a guide, series recordings and automatic commercial skipping.

Everyone gets their own

Profiles keep progress, favourites and settings apart. Turn on accounts for passwords, roles and signed-in device control.

Search

Find any movie, show, episode, channel or programme in a few letters, forgiving of spelling and punctuation.

Yours, not ours

One binary or container on your own hardware. No account with us and no tracking: it only talks to the services you set up, like OMDb for metadata and the HDHomeRun guide.

Run it

docker run -d --name couchside -p 8080:8080 \
  -v couchside-data:/data -v couchside-cache:/cache \
  -v /path/to/media:/media \
  ghcr.io/timothydodd/couchside:latest

Then open http://localhost:8080 and add your libraries. Zips for Linux, macOS and Windows, a Helm chart for Kubernetes and the full setup guide are in the README.
